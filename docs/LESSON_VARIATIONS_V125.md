# Teaching variations in V125

Five existing lessons gain a visible “Try a change with me” section, identified on their course maps. They add 21 selectable versions with full worked explanations, without adding formal units, quiz gates, streaks or assessment writes. All 87 V124 unit records, seven projects, seven workshops and 208 unrelated embedded assets remain exact. The seven reader assets share the new renderer; the other two courses display no extra section.

| Course and unit | What the learner can change | What the teacher shows |
|---|---|---|
| Piano, Make a phrase sound like one thought (`phrasing`) | Three replies to the same C–D–E opening | A C ending, an E ending and a delayed half-beat D; exact note/onset/duration tables, 40/60/80 BPM playback and why the pulse stays steady. Closure is described in the stated C-centred context, not as an absolute musical rule. |
| Spanish, Carry a café conversation (`cafe-project`) | One/two coffees, here/to go, with/without clarification: eight exchanges | The complete Spanish and English script, agreement in dos cafés pequeños, an either/or reply and the change from puede to puedo. Whole/slow/line audio uses an available device voice; the complete written lesson remains when no voice exists. |
| Chess, Build a mating net (`queen-net`) | Qb7, Qb6 or Qa5+ from the same composed position | Checkmate, stalemate and …Kb8 respectively. Before/after views and the actual legal king reply by button or board click show all three neighboring squares. This is a worked ending, not a chess engine. |
| Calculus, The slope hidden in everyday life (`calculus-1`) | Four one-second intervals of s(t)=t², with t in seconds and s in metres | Endpoint positions on one fixed track, matching clock readings, subtraction and division giving average velocities 1, 3, 5 and 7 m/s. These interval averages are not presented as unchanging instantaneous velocities. |
| Physics, Draw the forces (`force-diagrams`) | A 10 N, 4 N or zero push on a rightward-sliding 2 kg cart | Signed force sums 6, 0 and −4 N, hence accelerations 3, 0 and −2 m/s². Balanced forces retain the starting velocity. The fixed 4 N kinetic-friction snapshot ends when rightward sliding ends; it does not predict a spontaneous reversal. |

The original worked examples and demonstrations remain. Each extension ends with an optional real-world or spoken variation, and the learner can continue without answering anything. The text explains the decisions rather than congratulating the learner for clicking a control.

Source: `lesson-variations-v125.json`, `.js`, `.css`, connected by the existing reader/builder. Each course embeds only its own authored variation data. The shared media generation/stop mechanism cancels asynchronous audio and speech when changing the version, unit or page. The piano player schedules its own scoped model without duplicating the original player's IDs. Spanish line callbacks cannot restart an older exchange after a choice changes. No progress fields or storage keys are added. The V124 backup format remains compatible; exports identify the current app edition.

`qa/check-lesson-variations.py` independently checks every chess move and complete reply set using python-chess, all note/bar durations, rate and force arithmetic, exact V124 course records and unrelated archive assets. `qa/test-lesson-variations.cjs` exercises all 21 versions, actual board replies and keyboard focus, note frequencies/onsets/durations, cancellation while AudioContext resumes, speech sequencing and missing-voice behavior. Twenty full scoped axe scans cover all five sections at 320/390 px in both themes. Assessment records must remain unchanged. These join the complete regression suite in `npm test`.

Offline verification disables browser networking after the initial HTML load and opens all five readers. Direct file-URL navigation is blocked by the managed Chromium policy in this workspace, so an actual file-URL test is not claimed. Public Vercel HTTP requests are also blocked by the workspace proxy; publication is verified through deployment status and source-file hashes, while actual HTML/ZIP downloads are tested on local HTTP.
