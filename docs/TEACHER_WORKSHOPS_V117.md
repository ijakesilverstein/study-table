# V117 — practice and decisions taught in full

V117 adds a teacher workshop to the piano, Spanish and chess course maps and sidebars. Each workshop has four chapters with complete reasoning, a demonstration and prerequisite notes. The workshops are separate additions; all 65 earlier units, their 130 worked cases, the five projects, maths help, original studios and Claude additions remain. No quiz gate or mastery credit is added.

Piano works on the join between bars 2 and 3 of the original eight-bar piece. Ten playback patterns include an explicitly marked early-F error, the correct two-beat C, right/left isolation, arrival pairs, a reduced skeleton, partial restoration, the full join and four-bar context. The beat grid identifies attacks, holds and rests for each hand, with its explanation outside the horizontally scrollable table. The optional keyboard stays collapsed until needed. A four-click count-in and quiet pulse support 40–80 BPM practice. Individual keyboard notes still sound immediately. The old player’s defaults remain unchanged outside these opt-in models.

Spanish has thirteen authored, translated model exchanges. Chapters connect quiero/quieres/queremos to the person, place no before puedo/podemos, distinguish location/day/clock-time questions, and build voy a/vas a/vamos a plus a base-form action. A fourth future model explains how to leave a plan unsettled. Clickable phrase chunks explain their jobs; they do not mark answers. Device voice playback includes slow and line listening; changing models cancels old speech callbacks, and missing voices leave the script readable. No pronunciation judgment is claimed.

Chess uses four composed positions with two continuations each. Nc7+ forks king and rook, while a quiet knight move gives the rook time to move. Rxd5 followed by ...exd5 demonstrates the complete −2 material exchange. An absolutely pinned rook can capture its attacker on e8 or stay on the e-file with Re3; a sideways Rd2 is independently checked as illegal and is not animated. Qb7 is checkmate and Qb6 is stalemate in the supplied ending. All moves, FENs, target attacks and stated outcomes are independently verified with python-chess. Chess glyphs are explicitly sized independently of general button text, and board cells retain square corners and a square aspect ratio on phones. The prose does not present a quiet alternative as an engine-approved best move.

Thirty-six worked-step controls bring the existing workshop model beside the written reasoning, with a return-to-top control. Course and main Continue restore the saved workshop chapter. Existing unit/project reading places and aliases remain compatible. Reading uses the existing per-subject key with value workshop; the new :workshop-part key stores 0–3. Parent metadata has a separate workshopId only for the three relevant subjects. The reader API exposes openWorkshop and workshop metadata. Assessment records are unchanged.

Sources are teacher-workshops-v117.json/.js/.css and workshop-demos-v117.json. The development-only generator is qa/generate-workshop-demos.py. Production builds read committed JSON and need no chess package.

qa/check-workshops.py compares every earlier V116 field, validates all chapters, and independently checks the board lines, fork, exchange balance, pin legality, mate/stalemate and music timing. qa/test-workshops.cjs reads every chapter, follows every model link, checks all thirteen phrase models, queue cancellation, count-ins, early-versus-counted timing, immediate note samples, asynchronous audio cancellation, saved places, mobile overflow, both themes and unchanged assessments. Existing regression and local online/download delivery checks remain required. Vercel readiness, protection and uploaded hashes are verified through its API; public HTTP checks remain blocked by this workspace’s proxy.
