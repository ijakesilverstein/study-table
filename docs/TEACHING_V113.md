## Changes

- **Spanish past viewpoint:** replace the misleading era/estaba/tenía heading with the actual topic. Four selectable views show whole-event reporting, ongoing background, an explicit progressive form and transfer to cooking. Each includes a translation, three sentence parts with their jobs, a viewpoint sketch and replay. Selecting a new view cancels prior audio; the written teaching remains available without a Spanish voice.
- **Piano intervals and chords:** count moves through every key, starting from zero. Compare C–D, E–F, C–E and D–F with matching rise-and-return playback. Show the two root-position C-major gaps: four semitones then three. Explain why letter-name distance, pitch gap, inversion and arpeggiation describe different features.
- **Product rule:** draw the old rectangle, both added strips and the corner. Exact finite changes over 0.5, 0.1 and 0.01 seconds give interval rates 19.75, 16.71 and 16.0701 m²/s, approaching the instantaneous 16. This connects the geometry to the limiting calculation instead of multiplying the two side rates.
- **Energy:** compare 2 kg and 4 kg sleds with zero or a specified 36 J thermal transfer. Final kinetic plus thermal energy matches the initial gravitational amount. Explain why mass cancels in the frictionless model and why a fixed energy-loss amount is a different comparison.
- **Momentum:** show four approaching-cart setups before and after sticking, including leftward total momentum. Keep signed momentum and kinetic-energy accounting visible together. Before-view positions and velocity arrows must depict carts moving toward a collision.
- **Chess:** the first full-course movement diagram covers all six piece types, both pawn directions and a forward blocker. Its second worked case contrasts forward moves with diagonal captures. Detailed en passant moves out of the opening lesson into an optional supplement beside promotion. One legal line removes the adjacent pawn; a pinned-pawn counterexample stays a diagram because the capture would expose the king.
