## Review and repairs

The original extracted content remains in claude-additions-v114.json. A separate claude-corrections-v114.json records targeted refinements: español has no stress accent; cuándo is broader than a day; a pin does not forbid every move along its line; a constant velocity term can remain after differentiating a position rule; longer note values do not universally make beat and attack counts unequal. Introductory review prompts now supply unfamiliar vocabulary or teach the extra keyboard landmarks before asking about them.

The figure module retains every imported topic while repairing its models: exact finite changes in the product-rule rectangle, actual circle growth at a fixed diagram scale, a garden wall along the correct side for 2x+y=20, negative force arrows, a current graph without the artificial 12 A cap, consistent input bounds for time intervals and thermal transfer, and a fixed number of force markers as contact area narrows. Wave claims stay tied to the specified constant-speed model.

The practice area has one optional disclosure and independent show/hide buttons for its two answers. Reading and the full worked examples remain available before practice. The new controls and review do not write assessment scores. Richer V113 panels, audio cancellation, bookmarks, merged-unit aliases and practice returns remain available.
