# V112 curriculum edit

Request: refine, develop, expand and trim the curricula. This pass edits the five written foundation courses; the original practice applications and other subjects remain available.

## Cut repetition and keep the teaching

Thirteen explanation sections were rewritten. Their teaching arrays decreased from 2,262 to 1,102 whitespace-delimited words, a cut of 1,160 words. This is a count of those edited explanation sections, not the entire app. Numerical steps that already appeared in a worked case were replaced with shorter explanations of the mechanism or the conditions that make the method valid.

Examples: the instantaneous-rate explanation now distinguishes a limit from substituting zero instead of repeating the table of rates; the free-fall explanation says why the endpoint average requires constant acceleration; the circuit introduction separates energy per charge from charge per time. Staff reading focuses on landmarks and interval shape. Conversation repair distinguishes repetition, slower speech and asking for meaning.

Two full-course units were folded into related units. Piano `performance` becomes a concrete four-step performance project inside `read-melody`. Chess `threats` becomes the contrasting second case inside `chess-1`: an attacked rook with a safe king, after the first case about answering check. Its board line remains selectable. Both old unit IDs resolve to the combined lessons in saved reading places, incoming hashes and course Continue.

The net result is 65 units and 130 worked cases: Spanish 15, piano 14, chess 14, calculus 11 and physics 11. Each course map has three named chapters with a concrete purpose. Calculus now places optimization with derivative applications before the integration chapter.
