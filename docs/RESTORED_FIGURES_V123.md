# V123: recover twelve omitted Claude V120 figures

The uploaded opportunities brief led to a comparison with the original Claude V120. That comparison found twelve subject-specific interactive figures omitted from the V121 merge and still absent in V122. The earlier statement that every Claude addition was preserved was too broad. The 87 written unit records were retained, but checking their JSON and the sixteen older figure definitions did not establish preservation of the new executable figure features.

This release restores all twelve incoming figure IDs and their teaching purposes. The exact incoming blocks remain archived; corrected runtime implementations revise the calculations, model explanations and phone presentation. All 87 V122 unit records, seven projects, seven workshops and the other 213 embedded assets remain unchanged. The five original course assets remain byte-equivalent to V122; only medicine and business course assets change inside the archive.

## Corrected teaching and calculations

- **Feedback:** explicitly plot deviation from a fixed target against model steps. Reduce error by a factor of 0.62 or amplify by 1.30. A zero starting deviation stays zero under either rule. Adaptive axes show the complete curve instead of silently clipping it; these are toy update rules, not physiological forecasts.
- **Blood pressure:** identify the one-third pulse-pressure formula as an approximation to mean arterial pressure. Compare diastolic, approximate MAP, midpoint and systolic on a common scale, without claiming a schematic waveform supplies a true measured mean. The systolic slider's minimum and actual value stay at least 10 mmHg above diastolic, and its visible output/aria value match the calculation.
- **Kidney balance:** track water volume under fixed daily filtration/reabsorption assumptions; do not generalize that balance to every solute. Reabsorbed plus excreted volume equals filtered volume. The shared-scale chart keeps the small urine volume honest and the table supplies exact displayed values.
- **Half-life:** distinguish elapsed hours, elapsed half-lives, fraction and amount. State single-start/constant-first-order assumptions. Define an illustrative 25 mg absolute threshold when discussing how doubling the starting amount adds one half-life. Time to the same fraction is unchanged. Tiny positive amounts remain visible in scientific notation rather than rounding to zero. The graph's point follows the selected elapsed time.
- **Test interpretation:** show expected counts, with fractions allowed, under the selected prevalence/sensitivity/specificity. Keep denominators explicit and compute both positive and negative predictive values. A zero positive or negative total produces an undefined corresponding predictive value, rather than a fabricated zero. The description no longer claims the test stayed unchanged after the learner edits its sensitivity or specificity.
- **Gas exchange:** compare the product area × gradient / thickness with a stated reference, not with a percentage of health or oxygen saturation. Display rates above 100% without capping the bar. Qualify the relationship between ventilation, partial-pressure difference and actual oxygen transfer.
- **Unit economics:** distinguish negative contribution from zero contribution, and contribution from profit after fixed costs. Let costs exceed price without drawing a clipped price-scaled cost bar.
- **Breakeven:** align the introduction with the actual rising contribution and flat fixed-cost lines. Check the rounded whole-box threshold and the preceding box, including exact-integer breakeven.
- **Cash cycle:** keep the dated batch timeline, but calculate operating balances separately: receivables at sales, inventory/payables at cost. At monthly sales 20,000, variable costs 13,000, inventory 40 days, customer terms 45 and supplier terms 30, operating working capital is 34,333.33, not sales/30 × 55 = 36,666.67. The model explicitly assumes 30-day months and all variable costs in the inventory/payables stream. A negative cycle does not, by itself, establish negative working capital or positive cash generation.
- **Growth:** replace the ambiguous cycle-days-only funding control with the separate inventory/customer/supplier timings. At 40,000 sales versus a 20,000 baseline under the preceding assumptions, profit is 5,000, additional balance funding 34,333.33, and the simplified cash bridge −29,333.33. Explain that a difference between standing balances is not an exact dated forecast for the transition month. Signed bars use position, signs and a table rather than color alone.
- **Pricing:** for a price rise, permitted volume loss is 1 − old contribution/new contribution. With 40 price, 26 variable cost and a 10% increase, this is 22.22%, replacing the original −28.57% result. A 10% price cut needs 40% more volume. Handle unchanged price, zero contribution and negative contribution separately.
- **Discounting:** reverse compound growth by division, and check with the unrounded present value. Distinguish rounded display values from the internal calculation and avoid describing subtraction of a simple percentage as the inverse of compound growth. The graph's point follows the selected year.

## Source and preservation

`source/imports/claude-v120-medicine-figures.js` and `claude-v120-business-figures.js` are exact extracts from the unchanged original V120 HTML. They are preserved for inspection and are not executed by the build. `source/restored-figures-v123.json` records hashes, incoming IDs and corrected runtime filenames. The complete original remains `source/imports/claude-v120-original.html`, SHA256 `b197ad688aa40235a6441cc8b6fc6cf6121152e6620908258f525c9611bd7734`.

Runtime source is in `restored-medicine-v123.js`, `restored-business-v123.js`, `restored-figure-reader-v123.js` and `restored-figures-v123.css`. Only the corresponding subject module and style are embedded in each of the two affected readers. Existing figure controls keep their `data-v114` IDs. The dedicated renderer supplies compact SVGs, responsive semantic tables, explicit assumptions and polite calculation announcements. It delegates all older figures to the existing renderer. Neither exploration nor self-reading manufactures assessed success or changes the assessment store.
