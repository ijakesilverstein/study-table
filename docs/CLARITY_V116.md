# V116 — show the step where it is explained

The V115 projects supplied complete solutions, but the interactive demonstration sat above the written reasoning. V116 links all sixty worked steps to the corresponding demonstration state and brings the same demonstration beside the chosen paragraph. No project, unit or worked-case record from V115 is changed.

Piano steps select and play the hand and exact bar range being taught, including single bars, with the existing count-in and cancellation controls. Spanish steps show the relevant exchange, highlight the authored reply and play that line; the café walkthrough resets to its stated worked example before adapting. Chess steps jump to the exact recorded position. Calculus steps set the stated tank/time/capacity, including the exact filling instant and its nonexistent ordinary derivative. Physics steps select the stated height and stage.

A maths help desk addresses calculations the courses otherwise assume. Seven topics each contain two complete examples: fraction division/common factors; balancing equations; squares/roots/exact fractions; quadratics via completing the square and factoring; graph slope versus area; signs/units; and term-by-term derivatives/accumulation with an initial condition. New symbols and necessary terms are explained. Contextual buttons open the topic used in a project step or existing quantitative unit. The learner can choose another topic and close the dialog with its button or Escape, returning focus to the opening control. Help neither grades nor changes assessment records.
