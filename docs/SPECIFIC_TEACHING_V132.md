# V132: specific teaching and worked references

This release merges concurrent `feature/v131-unified-tutor` through 36937ba9b624ecb026af6bbbc2738095e720fc68 and `feature/v132-curiosity-grounded` through 67fc6d803e9636d571d71088fd2badf1dd679ca7, including curiosity features, direct checked-tutor entry and mobile table/dialog fixes. V130's retained working notes, step synchronization, audio support and local dictation safeguards stay in place. Earlier generated HTML editions remain available.

The main addition from this pass is authored teaching content. Eight lessons now have **51 individually written prompts, hints and explanations of sticking points**, aligned to their actual worked steps. They cover two-hand piano coordination, rests and subdivisions, Spanish noun groups and café repair, chess exchanges and castling, the product rule, and sled energy. Asking for help on the width strip, for example, explains the 4 m/s rate acting along the 3 m length. It no longer returns the same subject-wide nudge used for every calculus step.

Three further checked coaching scenarios cover a silent piano beat and half-beat timing, recovering a café question and confirming an order, and the two-piece movement and attack restrictions of castling. They add nine decisions and eighteen specific help branches to the earlier fifteen decisions. All eight checked coaches have a directly visible **Work this out with feedback** entry beside the first worked problem. Optional comparison of a learner's own reasoning remains available separately.

The three new scenarios show their worked references before an answer is required. Piano uses a beat/time/action table. Spanish keeps the complete exchange and translation beside each question, with Spanish language markup. Chess shows the before/after castling positions and the forbidden crossing-square example, with text descriptions. These diagrams are references; the original playable lesson boards and piano/audio demonstrations remain.

Spanish checks accept several natural repeat requests, takeout replies and confirmation forms. Unrecognized wording receives a comparison, not a claim that it is invalid Spanish. The checks distinguish the two-option question from confirmation and point out the accent in sí. New square checks distinguish the king's destination from the rook's and the crossing square. Numeric timing checks match the actual existing note schedules. Arbitrary working notes and musical performance are not graded.

The concurrent curiosity feature is retained as optional practice. Course maps can offer a real authored question near the current reading place, with its explanation available immediately. A project/workshop bookmark opens the full course pool. A thought experiment remains folded after the teaching, with a **Read the worked explanation** route that requires no choice. Only one claim is displayed; 39 units use a valid approach and 48 use one needing correction, so neither answer length nor always rejecting a claim provides the solution. Eight new concise valid/tempting pairs supply the selected claim and its explanation in the revised lessons. These activities do not write assessed success, progress or mastery.
